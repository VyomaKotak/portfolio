Chapter 03 — How I actually work

Three convictions, earned the hard way.

Four years on call for systems that couldn't go down left me with a few opinions about how machine learning should be built.

The failure modes come first

Before I pick a model I map what will break it: class imbalance, drift, train/serve skew, leakage. Design for those, and accuracy tends to look after itself.

A metric should cost something

Accuracy on imbalanced data is a comforting lie. I choose metrics that reflect real consequences, and I expose the threshold so the business owns the trade-off, not a default.

If I can't explain it, it isn't finished

A result nobody understands can't be trusted or acted on. SHAP, clear visuals, and plain language are part of the deliverable, not an extra.
