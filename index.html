About

Most data scientists have never had to explain a system failure to a client at 2am. I have — just with SAP, not a model.

I joined Tata Consultancy Services in 2021 and spent four years deep inside enterprise systems: SAP BASIS administration first, then Python development building web applications and REST APIs. Midnight upgrades, 6am incidents, stakeholders who needed answers before their morning coffee. It taught me how to stay calm when things break and to treat technology as something embedded inside real, messy, human systems.

That shift, from keeping systems alive to building the logic running on top of them, is where my move into data science began. Reliability, monitoring and thoughtful system design are habits for me, not afterthoughts I bolt on at the end.

The problems I enjoy most all come back to one question: how do we make sense of complex, noisy information so that better decisions follow? Lately that has pulled me toward the production edge of AI, LLMs, RAG and agentic workflows, because the gap between a working prototype and a system a business can rely on is exactly the kind of problem I find interesting.

Real-Time Fraud Detection Platform

GitHub ↗
PythonKafkaSpark Structured StreamingFastAPIStreamlitDocker
Problem
Fraud is only 0.13% of transactions. A model that predicts "never fraud" is 99.87% accurate and completely worthless. Transactions have to be judged in milliseconds, at thousands per second, without blocking real customers.
Approach
An end-to-end streaming platform scoring transactions through Kafka, Spark Structured Streaming, an ML model, FastAPI and a live Streamlit dashboard. A shared feature module runs across training, the API and the Spark scorer to eliminate train/serve skew, with the decision threshold exposed for the business to tune against a cost matrix.

Amazon Review Sentiment at Scale

GitHub ↗
PythonPySparkSpark MLlibTF-IDFSHAPTableau
Problem
43.9M Amazon Electronics reviews (21 GB) of unread feedback. At this scale pandas runs out of memory before it finishes loading the file, so everyday tooling is off the table.
Approach
A six-stage PySpark ML pipeline (Tokenizer, StopWords, TF-IDF, Scaler) benchmarking four classifiers with cross-validation.
